ENGINEERING PRINCIPLES

Designed for Quiet, Energy-Efficient Spaces

01 / THERMAL ENVELOPE

Hermetic Thermal Barrier

Multi-chamber profiles and polyamide thermal barriers significantly reduce heating and cooling HVAC loads, preventing temperature bridging and internal condensation.

02 / ACOUSTIC CONTROL

Precision Noise Damping

Continuous dual and triple EPDM compression gaskets combined with multi-point perimeter locks create hermetic chamber seals delivering up to 45dB acoustic suppression.

03 / STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY

Certified Wind Load Class C5

High-tensile structural aluminium and internal galvanized steel reinforcement provide high resistance against wind load and intense weather conditions in high-rise applications.
